# Box-family standards

Defaults for any new Middle-earth travel case. Reuse them unless the brief says otherwise. They are precedents for this box family, not universal printer tolerances.

| Item | Standard | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Magnets | 10 x 2 mm everywhere (David's preferred size). Pocket 10.20 x 2.15 mm, 0.85 mm roof | Pocket fit-tested on the lift key; lift strength on panels untested |
| Storage bins | Standard Gridfinity: 42 mm grid, 7 mm height units, 4.4 mm stacking lip. The Shire interior takes a 5 x 4 plate; bins up to 10 units with lip (7 under the connector shelf) | Digital only |
| Gridfinity magnets | Standard bin feet take only 6 x 2 mm; 10 x 2 mm does not fit | Known limit |
| Case envelope | 240 x 200 mm, base to seam 110.8 mm, lid 26.12 mm, 4 mm walls, 12 mm corner radius. Carved relief grows the footprint (Mirkwood base 260 x 221 mm); check the bed size | Printed |
| Lid | V2.1 r2: one-piece outer lid body plus removable light tray. The outer body STL is stored face-down | Printed |
| Lithophane pane | 204 x 154 mm, square corners, 200 x 150 visible, 0.8 to 2.0 mm thick | Printed |
| Lid connectors | Set B pair needs 11.84 mm between holder mounting faces; the base holder face is 8.92 mm below the seam, so the lid holder sits 2.92 mm above it (tray r3) | Tray r3 not yet printed |
| Feet | Separate printed stumps, glued, keyed by an asymmetric tab with a 0.2 mm gap, or a straight cylinder peg (6.0 mm peg, 6.5 mm hole) | Printed |
| Fonts | Luminari for carved text, Trattatello for lithophane text; override per box | Printed |
